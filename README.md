Hi, I'm Ishan. I study applied math and CS at UC Berkeley. I like problems where you decide under uncertainty and find out quickly whether you were right, like poker and markets.

Most of this is from fall 2026:

- [no-limit-pokerbot](https://github.com/ivannadil/no-limit-pokerbot): Aryan Mukherjee and I won the Bay Area regional of No Limit with this poker bot, first of 148 teams. No Limit is a bot tournament run by Sequoia and hosted by OpenAI and Citadel Securities. We tested the bot's settings on the real field during the scored rounds. In the national final we came 14th of 21, and the README gets into why.
- [coinflip-lab](https://github.com/ivannadil/coinflip-lab): a paper-trading lab where a strategy only counts if it beats its coin-flip twins, bots that trade as often and pay the same costs but choose at random. Every idea is committed to git before it's tested, and the held-out data gets one look. Ten of the first twelve ideas failed. The other two now log live decisions next to coin-flip control bots.
- [Distill](https://github.com/distillAI-maker/distillV1): a wellness app I co-founded that tells you which supplements and habits you can drop. It's TypeScript on top of the Claude API, and the iOS app is submitted to the App Store. I built the rules engine, the 156-row evidence table behind it, and its evaluation, which is 42 scripted users whose checks run in CI. ([distillthenoise.com](https://distillthenoise.com))
- [xTrain](https://github.com/ivannadil/xTrain): a soccer-training app for players who train alone, made by a team of five. I did the technical drill library and the movement scanner. ([live site](https://xtrain-soccer.vercel.app))
- [aux](https://github.com/ivannadil/aux): my own version of Spotify, more or less. It finds songs in Apple's catalog and plays them through YouTube's official player or from my own lossless files, and it syncs across my devices with a three-way merge and no server of its own. ([live](https://ivannadil.github.io/aux/))
