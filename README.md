Hi, I'm Ishan. I study applied math and computer science at UC Berkeley. I like problems where you decide under uncertainty and find out quickly whether you were right: poker, markets, and products that real people use.

### Selected work

**[No Limit pokerbot](https://github.com/ivannadil/no-limit-pokerbot).** My team won the Bay Area regional of No Limit, a poker-bot tournament run by Sequoia and hosted by OpenAI and Citadel Securities: first of 148 teams over six rounds. We tested the bot's settings on the real field inside the scored rounds. In the national final we came 14th of 21, and the README says why.

**[Coin-Flip Lab](https://github.com/ivannadil/coinflip-lab).** A paper-trading research lab where an idea has to beat coin-flip twins that trade as often and pay the same costs. Every hypothesis is committed before it is tested, and the lockbox gets one look. Ten of the first twelve ideas failed; two now log live decisions next to coin-flip control bots.

**[Distill](https://github.com/distillAI-maker/distillV1).** I co-founded a wellness app that tells you which supplements and habits you can drop (TypeScript, Claude API, iOS app submitted to the App Store). I built the rules engine, the 156-row evidence table behind it, and its evaluation: 42 scripted users whose checks run in CI. [distillthenoise.com](https://distillthenoise.com)

**[xTrain](https://github.com/ivannadil/xTrain).** A soccer-training app for players who train alone, built by a team of five. My part was the technical drill library and the movement scanner. [Live site](https://xtrain-soccer.vercel.app)

**[Aux](https://github.com/ivannadil/aux).** My own music player: Apple's catalog, YouTube's official player and my own lossless files, synced across my devices with a three-way merge and no server of its own. [Live](https://ivannadil.github.io/aux/)
